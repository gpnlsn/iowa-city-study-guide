Best Places to Study in Iowa City

Looking for a reliable place to get schoolwork done? These three fictional Iowa City locations are designed for different types of students, from people who like a little background activity to students who need a comfortable table for a long study session.

01 — RIVERBEND COFFEE HOUSE

Best Overall for a Long Study Session

Riverbend Coffee House is a fictional student-friendly café near downtown Iowa City. It has large tables, comfortable chairs, plenty of outlets, and a relaxed atmosphere that makes it easy to settle in for several hours. The space is especially useful when you want a little background noise without feeling distracted.

Best for Long study sessions
Atmosphere Relaxed and social
Study tip Grab a table near an outlet
02 — MAPLE STREET COMMONS

Best for Studying With Friends

Maple Street Commons is a fictional shared study café created for students who like working around other people. Its larger tables make it convenient for group projects, reviewing presentations, or comparing notes before an exam. The atmosphere is energetic enough to keep you motivated while still being comfortable for individual work.

Best for Group projects
Atmosphere Friendly and active
Study tip Visit with your project group
03 — OAK & PAGE

Best for Reading and Writing

Oak & Page is a fictional bookstore café where students can combine studying with a quieter reading environment. The space includes small tables and comfortable seating that work well for essays, textbook reading, and reviewing class notes. Its book-filled interior gives the location a focused academic feel.

Best for Reading and writing
Atmosphere Cozy and academic
Study tip Bring headphones for extra focus
